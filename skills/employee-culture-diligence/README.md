# Employee Culture Diligence

A reusable investor-oriented agent skill for extracting organizational signal from employee reviews and discussions.

## Trigger examples

- `Run employee culture diligence on Marvell.`
- `Run employee culture diligence on NVIDIA from an investment perspective.`
- `Compare Cadence and Synopsys employee cultures.`
- `Assess whether Astera Labs has a cultural execution advantage over Broadcom.`

## Core idea

The skill does not ask whether employees are happy. It asks what employees reveal—often unintentionally—about the organization's ability to execute, innovate, retain strategically important talent, respond to change, and create shareholder value.

The workflow deliberately separates:

1. **Current culture** — driven primarily by reviews from the last 24 months.
2. **Historical trajectory** — older reviews used to understand what changed and why.

It also prioritizes strategically relevant employee cohorts over headline workplace ratings.
