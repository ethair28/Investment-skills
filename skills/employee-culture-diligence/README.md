# Employee Culture Diligence

A reusable investor-oriented skill for extracting organizational signal from employee reviews and discussions.

The skill is designed to transfer an **investment mental model**, not enforce a fixed report format.

## Trigger examples

- `Run employee culture diligence on Marvell.`
- `Compare Cadence and Synopsys employee cultures.`
- `Does AMD have the organizational culture needed to close the software gap?`
- `Has Synopsys culture deteriorated over the last three years?`
- `Find public semiconductor companies whose employee evidence suggests unusually strong engineering cultures that investors may underappreciate.`
- `Find companies where mediocre headline employee ratings hide an exceptional strategic technical core.`

## Core idea

The skill does not ask whether employees are happy. It asks what employees reveal—often unintentionally—about the organization's ability to create shareholder value.

It teaches the agent to:

- separate employee sentiment from investor implications;
- prioritize strategically relevant employee cohorts over headline workplace averages;
- use recent evidence for current culture and older evidence primarily for trajectory;
- distinguish healthy intensity from dysfunction;
- search for counter-sentiment evidence and contradictions;
- recognize internal cultural dispersion across teams, business units, geographies, or acquired organizations;
- adapt the culture assessment to the company's competitive environment;
- use employee culture both for diligence and for investment idea discovery.

## Research modes

The skill can adapt to:

- single-company diligence;
- competitive comparisons;
- narrow organizational thesis questions;
- cultural trajectory or monitoring;
- discovery/screens for unusually strong or underappreciated organizational cultures;
- searches for hidden high-quality strategic cores or unusually credible leadership signals.
